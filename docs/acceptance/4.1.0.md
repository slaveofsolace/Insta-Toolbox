# 4.1.0 acceptance

This record separates source tests, generated-userscript browser fixtures, and
authenticated Instagram acceptance. The stable Tampermonkey channel changes
only when the release is published.

## DM Unsend restoration

- Standard traversal is the only single-conversation speed. Fast-mode state is
  rejected rather than interpreted.
- The native runner keeps visible-first traversal, adaptive 1–2 second pacing,
  sent-by-me proof, exact menu and confirmation matching, Stop settlement, and
  stable-exhaustion completion.
- After every verified removal, the walker resets its position even when
  Instagram recycles or reorders rows without changing the scroll height.
- Deterministic browser fixtures pass for direct chats, group chats, replies,
  story replies, reels, id-less messages, unavailable-account labels, received
  rows, virtualized history, and uncertain outcomes.
- A dispatched result that cannot be proved is reported as uncertain and is
  never clicked again automatically.

These checks are strong regression evidence, but they do not prove that the
current authenticated Instagram DOM is unchanged. A disposable multi-message
run in the exact 4.1.0 build remains the final compatibility gate.

## Ghost cleanup

The userscript reviews exact discovered thread IDs and may open one to five
managed worker tabs. Tabs can open in front or inactive. Workers prepare
conversations concurrently, while account mutations remain serialized through
one account-scoped lane and one pacing clock. Stop closes only tabs created by
that job. Coordinator loss, wrong account or thread, expiry, restriction,
storage failure, or an uncertain result prevents another mutation.

The reviewed userscript fixtures cover selection, worker ownership, serial
verified removals, Stop, and five rendered layouts including true 200% zoom.
Current authenticated multi-conversation acceptance is pending. Background
support means a loaded inactive tab; it does not mean a frozen, discarded,
closed, signed-out, sleeping, or browser-closed tab.

## Presence

Presence exposes five native-control activities: view stories, react to
stories, like posts, follow people, and accept incoming requests. One session
is bounded to 1–50 actions. **Live like me** is still finite: 30 minutes to 12
hours, at most 500 verified actions, and a chosen rest period after each chosen
burst.

Presence reaches Instagram sections through observed controls, not manufactured
routes. Its review is runtime-only, account-bound, one-use, and expiring.
Pause, Resume, and Stop fence the next action. Restrictions, changed context,
or an uncertain result halt the run.

A bounded per-account local log stores sanitized action, target, outcome, and
detail fields. The newest entries appear inline. A separate resizable read-only
window provides Download and Clear and contains no action controls. Tests cover
redaction, bounded history, duplicate rejection, storage failure recovery, and
separate-window rendering.

Focused generated-userscript browser acceptance passes nine gates across dark,
light, narrow, short-window, and true 200% zoom states. Authenticated acceptance
of the current Instagram controls remains separate.

## Authenticated gates

- Unsend disposable sent messages until stable exhaustion, then verify Stop and
  restart without repeats.
- Run Ghost against an exact disposable multi-conversation selection and prove
  no unselected conversation changes.
- Remove one disposable owned reaction while preserving the message and other
  reactions.
- Exercise each enabled Presence activity against approved disposable targets.
- Verify inactive-tab behavior, restriction handling, account changes, and the
  stable Tampermonkey update in a persistent profile.

No authenticated mutation is claimed by this record. Fixtures contain synthetic
content and automated checks do not change Instagram account data.
